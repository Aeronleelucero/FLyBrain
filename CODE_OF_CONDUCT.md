# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the FlyBrain community to be respectful, constructive,
and welcoming, regardless of background, experience, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, intellectual-property rights, and project boundaries.
- Focus discussion on improving the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, sharing private information, or knowingly submitting malicious or
confidential material is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through a GitHub private security advisory or directly
to the Official Repository maintainer. The maintainer may remove content, limit
participation, or take other action appropriate to the circumstances. Reports
will be handled with reasonable discretion.
